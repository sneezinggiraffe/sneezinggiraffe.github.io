SkinBurp Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 August 2026

SkinBurp is a completely free Minecraft skin creator with no ads. It does not require an account and does not upload your skin files. Editing, drafts, imports, and exports are processed on your device.

Files and local storage

Your current draft is stored locally on your device so that work can be restored. When you open or save a PNG, SkinBurp accesses only the file you select or create. Skin images are not sent to the developer or any analytics service.

External links

The optional donation link opens a third-party payment provider. Its privacy practices apply after you leave SkinBurp.

Children

SkinBurp does not request names, email addresses, account details, or other directly identifying information from children. Parents and guardians can clear local app data or uninstall the app to remove locally stored drafts.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when SkinBurp's data practices change. Questions can be sent through the support contact shown on the Google Play listing.
